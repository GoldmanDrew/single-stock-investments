## What this business is
Aehr Test Systems (NASDAQ: AEHR) designs, manufactures, and sells wafer-level burn-in and test systems, led by the FOX-XP multi-wafer test platform and WaferPak contactors. **[Fact]** FY2026 revenue was **$50.0 million**, down from **$59.0 million** in FY2025 after the SiC capacity boom faded. **[Fact]** FY2026 GAAP net loss was **$7.1 million**. **[Fact]** Q4 FY2026 revenue was **$18.8 million** with GAAP net income **$1.4 million**, and effective backlog reached about **$100.6 million**. **[Fact]** Cash and restricted cash were **$116.5 million** after a large equity raise. **[Inference]** The equity is a SiC/AI burn-in recovery option with much stronger order visibility than in the prior trough, but dilution and a still-rich price keep owner-cash returns deeply negative at spot until guidance converts to sustained free cash flow.

---

## Why the market might be wrong
The market may be pricing **FY2027 guidance ($130–$150 million revenue)** and the **$100 million effective backlog** while underweighting **FY2026 still-down year revenue**, **GAAP losses**, **customer concentration**, and **share count expansion** from the equity offering that funded the cash pile. **[Inference]** Order momentum is real; at **$109.89** the mid-cycle **$0.16 per share** owner-cash base still does not clear a return hurdle, and guidance must be earned in filings before it belongs in base cash.
