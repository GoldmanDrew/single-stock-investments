## What this business is

Astera Labs, Inc. (NASDAQ: ALAB) designs connectivity semiconductor solutions for cloud and AI infrastructure. **[Fact]** The company reports a **single operating segment** of connectivity ICs (PCIe, Ethernet, and CXL retimers and related products) sold to hyperscalers, OEMs, and ODMs. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$852.5 million**, up from **$396.3 million** in FY2024 and **$115.8 million** in FY2023 (XBRL tags in thousands). **[Fact]** FY2025 net income was **$219.1 million** (**$1.32** basic EPS); operating income **$173.4 million**; operating cash flow **$319.3 million**. **[Fact]** Cash and equivalents were **$167.6 million** at December 31, 2025 with **no long-term debt** tagged in XBRL extracts. **[Fact]** Q1 2026 revenue was **$308.4 million**, up from **$159.4 million** in Q1 2025. **[Inference]** ALAB earns when AI and cloud racks need more high-speed connectivity silicon per server; growth has been extraordinary, but customer concentration and competitive retimer alternatives cap moat durability.
