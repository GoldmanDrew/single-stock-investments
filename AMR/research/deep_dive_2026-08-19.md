## What this business is
Alpha Metallurgical Resources is a leading U.S. producer and exporter of metallurgical (coking) coal from Central Appalachia. **[Fact]** The company operates nineteen active mines and eight coal preparation plants with approximately 3,960 employees, shipping met coal to domestic and international steelmakers. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$2.13 billion**, net loss **$61.7 million**, and operating cash flow **$144.9 million** per `AMR/investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-K_20260227_rpt20251231_acc0001704715_26_000010.htm`. **[Fact]** The company shipped **13.7 million tons** of met coal in 2025 and reported **294.5 million tons** of reserves (282.8 million proven and probable) at year-end. **[Fact]** At June 30, 2026 the balance sheet showed **$435.8 million** of cash (including restricted), **$3.0 million** of long-term debt, and **$1.50 billion** of stockholders' equity on **12.68 million** shares outstanding per `AMR/investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-Q_20260807_rpt20260630_acc0001704715_26_000031.htm`.

## Why the market might be wrong
The stock may embed a full met coal recovery while our normalized component base values trough-to-midcycle owner economics near **$63 per share** versus a price near **$169**. **[Inference]** H1 2026 revenue of **$1.02 billion** and operating cash flow of **$69 million** look better than FY2025 trough, and generalists may extrapolate that rebound into a sustained up-cycle. **[Inference]** The market may also credit **$433 million** of net cash and a shrinking share count without penalizing **$1.14 billion** of FY2025 buybacks funded as owner cash collapsed. **[Inference]** Conversely, if Alpha's low-cost CAPP position and reserve life survive a prolonged trough better than smaller peers, the bear case on absolute liquidity risk may be too harsh; dhando is partial, not full.

## Executive summary

Alpha Metallurgical Resources is the largest U.S. exporter of metallurgical coal from Central Appalachia, with **294.5 million tons** of reserves and **$436 million** of net cash at June 2026. FY2025 was a cycle trough: net loss **$62 million**, operating cash flow **$145 million**, yet the company repurchased **$1.14 billion** of stock. H1 2026 shows partial recovery but not a full mid-cycle run-rate. At **$169.44**, our trough-to-midcycle owner-cash model implies about **-11.57%** per year over seven years, and filing-normalized component economics near **$63 per share** sit far below price. Dhando is partial given net cash, but cyclical pricing and buyback timing prevent a fat-pitch verdict. We propose **watch** pending a human capital decision.
