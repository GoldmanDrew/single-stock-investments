## What this business is

Alpha Metallurgical Resources is a leading U.S. producer and exporter of metallurgical (coking) coal from Central Appalachia. **[Fact]** The company operates nineteen active mines and eight coal preparation plants with approximately 3,960 employees, shipping met coal to domestic and international steelmakers. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$2.13 billion**, net loss **$61.7 million**, and operating cash flow **$144.9 million** per `AMR/investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-K_20260227_rpt20251231_acc0001704715_26_000010.htm`. **[Fact]** The company shipped **13.7 million tons** of met coal in 2025 and reported **294.5 million tons** of reserves (282.8 million proven and probable) at year-end. **[Fact]** At June 30, 2026 the balance sheet showed **$435.8 million** of cash (including restricted), **$3.0 million** of long-term debt, and **$1.50 billion** of stockholders' equity on **12.68 million** shares outstanding per `AMR/investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-Q_20260807_rpt20260630_acc0001704715_26_000031.htm`.

## Why the market might be wrong

The stock may embed a full met coal recovery while our normalized component base values trough-to-midcycle owner economics near **$63 per share** versus a price near **$153**. **[Inference]** H1 2026 revenue of **$1.02 billion** and operating cash flow of **$69 million** look better than FY2025 trough, and generalists may extrapolate that rebound into a sustained up-cycle. **[Inference]** The market may also credit **$433 million** of net cash and a shrinking share count without penalizing **$1.14 billion** of FY2025 buybacks funded as owner cash collapsed. **[Inference]** Conversely, if Alpha's low-cost CAPP position and reserve life survive a prolonged trough better than smaller peers, the bear case on absolute liquidity risk may be too harsh; dhando is partial, not full.

## Risks & inversion

**Primary risk:** Met coal prices fail to sustain H1 2026 levels, trapping owner cash near FY2025 while management continues repurchases.

**Inversion checklist:**
- If H2 2026 operating cash flow annualizes below **$100 million**, trough normalization was too optimistic.
- If buybacks exceed **75%** of trailing twelve-month operating cash flow for two consecutive quarters, capital return is destroying trough liquidity.
- If met coal shipped falls below **12 million tons** for a full year without a disclosed mine closure plan, cost-curve advantage is eroding.

**Lens failure mode (capital cycle):** Capitalizing spot or near-spot margins after a trough rebound, or ignoring buybacks funded from prior-cycle cash, the classic peak-margin and capital-return failure modes for the capital_cycle profile.
