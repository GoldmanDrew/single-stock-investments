## What this business is
ASML Holding (NASDAQ: ASML) designs and manufactures lithography systems that pattern silicon wafers for advanced semiconductors. **[Fact]** The company is the sole commercial supplier of extreme ultraviolet (EUV) scanners at leading-edge nodes; deep ultraviolet (DUV) systems serve mature and mid-range layers. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **€32.7 billion** (about **$38.4 billion** at year-end FX) and normalized owner earnings (operating cash flow minus capital spending, converted to USD) were **$13.0 billion** on the locked fact ledger. **[Fact]** At December 31, 2025 the group held **€12.9 billion** of cash and **€2.7 billion** of long-term debt. **[Inference]** Customers are a concentrated set of foundries and memory makers; system shipments and service drive a cyclical but structurally scarce franchise.

---

## Why the market might be wrong
The market may be paying for a long EUV and High-NA upgrade cycle while underpricing **China export-control risk**, **memory and logic capex downturns**, and **capital intensity** that leaves normalized owner cash well below headline operating cash flow. **[Inference]** At **$1,736** (Yahoo close 2026-07-20), the contract base component value of **$644 per share** implies a negative base annual return near **-13%** per year on the automated owner-earnings reinvestment path, so the price embeds sustained reinvestment at high incremental returns that filings have not yet proven through a full down-cycle. **[Inference]** FY2025 cash generation was strong, but prior trough years (2023 owner cash was weaker) show the cycle can compress faster than the base DCF assumes.

## Risks & inversion
**Primary risk:** Export controls or a sharp foundry/memory capex cut collapse normalized owner earnings below the low-case bridge (typed falsifier draft pending review).

- Customer concentration: a handful of fabs drive the majority of system revenue.
- China revenue restriction can remove a large end market abruptly.
- Technology transitions (High-NA) can slip, delaying upgrade revenue.
- Cycle mean reversion: FY2025 peak earnings may not persist through the seven-year model horizon.

**Lens failure mode (quality_reinvestment):** If incremental reinvestment returns fall toward low-case **12%** after tax with **20%** reinvestment, owner-earnings growth slows and terminal value compresses toward the **$382** per share low case.
