## What this business is

American Express is a global payments and card-issuing franchise built around premium cardmembers, merchant acceptance, and a closed-loop network. **[Fact]** The company earns revenue from discount fees on billed business, net card fees, and interest on card loans. **[Fact]** FY2025 total revenues net of interest expense were **$72.2 billion**, up 10% versus the prior year (`investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-K_20260206_rpt20251231_acc0000004962_26_000080.htm`). **[Fact]** First-half 2026 revenues net of interest expense were **$38.5 billion** versus **$34.8 billion** in the prior-year period, a 10.7% increase (`investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-Q_20260724_rpt20260630_acc0000004962_26_000322.htm`). Stephen Squeri is Chairman and CEO.

---

## Why the market might be wrong

There is no dated payoff or structural discount here. **[Inference]** FY2025 and first-half 2026 show double-digit revenue growth and record net card fees, yet the stock trades near **$352** per share where our base intrinsic value is **$413** per share but the annual return at that price is only **2.3%** per year. **[Inference]** The market may be paying for a premium franchise while embedding credit-loss and rate-cycle risk that our conservative reinvestment model already caps. Return is earnings power at price, not a mispricing signal with a known catalyst date.

## Executive summary

American Express operates a multi-sided payment network serving affluent consumers and small businesses. FY2025 filings show **$72.2 billion** in revenues net of interest expense, **$18.4 billion** in cash from operations, and **$16.0 billion** in normalized owner earnings after capital spending. Net card fee revenue reached a record **$10 billion** with 18% year-over-year growth, and the first half of 2026 continued the pattern with **$38.5 billion** in revenues net of interest expense. At **$351.93** per share (Yahoo close, 2026-07-20), we expect about **2.3% per year** over seven years on normalized owner earnings reinvested at bounded returns. Asymmetric payoff (Pabrai dhando) is **partial** because credit losses and funding costs can compress owner cash in a downturn. The primary falsifier is trailing normalized owner earnings falling below the **$16.0 billion** FY2025 anchor. We propose **watch** until price offers a higher annual return or reinvestment proof tightens.

## Risks & inversion

**Primary risk:** **Credit-loss cycle** if consumer delinquencies rise and provision expense absorbs the card fee and discount revenue growth visible in FY2025 and H1 2026 filings.

- **Funding-cost pressure** if deposit and wholesale rates stay elevated versus card loan yields.  
- **Competitive rewards inflation** erodes net card fee uplift and merchant economics.  
- **Regulatory action** on interchange or credit practices compresses take rates.

**Inversion:** If we are wrong, American Express remains a premium payment franchise generating mid-teens billions in owner cash, but at **$352** per share the stock may deliver low single-digit returns rather than a broken business.

**Lens failure mode (operating):** Thesis fails if billed-business growth stalls for several years while credit costs rise and incremental returns on reinvestment fall below our **12%** low-case assumption.

#### IRR arithmetic (show your work)

How we calculated the annual return (base case). Verify with `python3 _system/scripts/marvin_valuation.py --ticker AXP --write`

1. **Price today:** **$351.93**
2. **Starting normalized owner earnings:** **$16,003 million** (FY2025 cash from operations minus capital spending)
3. **Reinvestment growth:** **6.3%** per year for seven years (**35%** reinvested × **18%** incremental return)
4. **Year-7 owner earnings:** **$16,003M** × (1.063)^7 ≈ **$24,543 million**
5. **Year-7 distributable cash:** **$24,543M** × **65%** ≈ **$15,953 million**
6. **Present value of seven years of distributable cash** at **10%** discount rate ≈ **$63,649 million**
7. **Terminal value:** **$24,543M** × **18** = **$441,781 million**; present value ≈ **$226,703 million**
8. **Enterprise value:** **$63,649M** + **$226,703M** = **$290,352 million**
9. **Equity value:** **$290,352M** + cash **$45,243M** − debt **$57,017M** = **$278,578 million**
10. **Base intrinsic value per share:** **$278,578M** ÷ **675.3M** shares = **$412.52**
11. **Annual return at today's price:** (**$412.52** ÷ **$351.93**)^(1/7) − 1 = **2.3%** per year (`valuation_contract.json` → `annualized_return_at_price_pct.base`)

**Upside / downside from price:** Low case **-6.0%** per year if owner earnings and reinvestment fall to the low bridge (**$228.65** per share); high case **12.2%** per year if reinvestment and terminal economics reach the high case (**$786.44** per share).
