## What this business is

Bitdeer Technologies is a Singapore-headquartered operator of bitcoin mining and AI computing infrastructure listed on Nasdaq as BTDR. **[Fact]** The company runs ten datacenters across the United States, Norway, Bhutan, Ethiopia, and Malaysia with **1,744 megawatts** of electrical capacity as of March 31, 2026. **[Fact]** Proprietary hash rate was **70.9 exahash per second** and total hash rate under management was **78.1 EH/s** on that date. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$620.3 million**, up from **$349.8 million** in FY2024, driven by self-mining (**$396 million**, 64% of revenue) and the first full year of proprietary **SEALMINER** rig sales (**$108 million**, 18%). **[Fact]** The company also offers hosting, cloud hash rate, and an early AI cloud business (**$6.8 million** revenue in FY2025 with **2,128 GPUs** deployed at **94% utilization** as of March 31, 2026).

## Why the market might be wrong

**Predictive attribute:** complexity discount (GAAP earnings and operating cash flow are dominated by convertible-derivative marks, SEALMINER inventory build, and an unproven AI pivot).

**[Inference]** The market may treat Bitdeer as a levered bitcoin proxy and reward FY2025 revenue scale-up while underweighting that reported operating cash flow was **negative $1.7 billion** because of **$787 million** of SEALMINER inventory build and **$408 million** of prepayments, not because mining suddenly became cash-negative at the margin. **[Fact]** Adjusted EBITDA was only **$35.2 million** on **$620 million** of revenue while net interest expense reached **$91.7 million** and the company carried roughly **$1.55 billion** of convertible notes. **[Inference]** Bulls may conversely underprice vertical integration: proprietary ASICs, **1.3 gigawatts** of pipeline capacity, and a live AI cloud footprint if bitcoin stabilizes and SEALMINER working capital normalizes.

## Risks & inversion

**Primary risk:** Bitcoin price and hashprice fall faster than Bitdeer can cut power cost or monetize SEALMINER inventory, leaving thin adjusted EBITDA unable to cover **$91.7 million** of net interest and convert obligations.

Additional risks:

- **~$1.55 billion** convertible note stack with loss-on-extinguishment charges (**$60.4 million** FY2025).
- SEALMINER inventory and prepayment build (**$1.2 billion+** working capital swing per 20-F liquidity MD&A).
- Cloud hash rate revenue collapse suggests legacy product obsolescence.
- Putative securities class action (2025) and Feb 2026 equity raise dilution at **$7.94** net tangible book.
- AI datacenter conversion competes with hyperscalers; GPU supply and regulatory risk.

**Lens failure mode (operating + optionality):** Using GAAP net income (**$65.6 million**) or revenue growth alone while ignoring that operating cash flow was **negative $1.7 billion**; treating Adjusted EBITDA as owner cash without subtracting sustaining capital spending or interest.

**Inversion:** What would make this work? Sustained bitcoin above prior cycle highs, SEALMINER third-party sales at hardware margins, working capital release from inventory normalization, and contracted AI/colocation cash flow on **1.7 GW** of power footprint.
