## What this business is

Bitdeer Technologies is a Singapore-headquartered operator of bitcoin mining and AI computing infrastructure listed on Nasdaq as BTDR. **[Fact]** The company runs ten datacenters across the United States, Norway, Bhutan, Ethiopia, and Malaysia with **1,744 megawatts** of electrical capacity as of March 31, 2026. **[Fact]** Proprietary hash rate was **70.9 exahash per second** and total hash rate under management was **78.1 EH/s** on that date. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$620.3 million**, up from **$349.8 million** in FY2024, driven by self-mining (**$396 million**, 64% of revenue) and the first full year of proprietary **SEALMINER** rig sales (**$108 million**, 18%). **[Fact]** The company also offers hosting, cloud hash rate, and an early AI cloud business (**$6.8 million** revenue in FY2025 with **2,128 GPUs** deployed at **94% utilization** as of March 31, 2026).

---

## Why the market might be wrong

**Predictive attribute:** complexity discount (GAAP earnings and operating cash flow are dominated by convertible-derivative marks, SEALMINER inventory build, and an unproven AI pivot).

**[Inference]** The market may treat Bitdeer as a levered bitcoin proxy and reward FY2025 revenue scale-up while underweighting that reported operating cash flow was **negative $1.7 billion** because of **$787 million** of SEALMINER inventory build and **$408 million** of prepayments, not because mining suddenly became cash-negative at the margin. **[Fact]** Adjusted EBITDA was only **$35.2 million** on **$620 million** of revenue while net interest expense reached **$91.7 million** and the company carried roughly **$1.55 billion** of convertible notes.

**[Inference]** The Aug 2026 **at-the-market** equity program (up to **$1 billion** on the existing sales agreement) may be read as flexible balance-sheet optionality, but filing math shows **227.4 million** Class A shares already outstanding at June 30, 2026 plus **3.6 million** shares sold for about **$50.3 million** gross before the supplement date. That is standing dilution overhang on thin normalized owner cash, not a free catalyst.

## Executive summary

At recent prices near **$10.88** (last reported in the Aug 2026 prospectus supplement), the base case still implies a **negative** annual return over seven years on normalized owner cash of about **$0.04 per share**. Bitdeer scaled proprietary hash to **70.9 EH/s** and **$620 million** of FY2025 revenue, but filing-based owner economics remain thin once SEALMINER inventory distortion is removed. GAAP net income of **$65.6 million** is not repeatable owner cash because it embeds a **$444.9 million** fair-value gain on derivative liabilities. Marvin proposes **watch** until either price offers a mid-teens annual return on normalized owner cash or filings show durable hash-margin improvement plus contracted AI or colocation revenue.

## Risks & inversion

**Primary risk:** Bitcoin price and hashprice fall faster than Bitdeer can cut power cost or monetize SEALMINER inventory, leaving thin adjusted EBITDA unable to cover **$91.7 million** of net interest and convert obligations.

Additional risks:

- **~$1.55 billion** convertible note stack with loss-on-extinguishment charges (**$60.4 million** FY2025).
- **Up to $1 billion** ATM equity program (Aug 2026) plus **3.6 million** shares already sold for ~**$50.3 million** gross after June 30, 2026.
- SEALMINER inventory and prepayment build (**$1.2 billion+** working capital swing per 20-F liquidity MD&A).
- Cloud hash rate revenue collapse suggests legacy product obsolescence.
- Putative securities class action (2025) and prior Feb 2026 equity raise dilution at **$7.94** net tangible book.
- AI datacenter conversion competes with hyperscalers; GPU supply and regulatory risk.

**Lens failure mode (operating + optionality):** Using GAAP net income (**$65.6 million**) or revenue growth alone while ignoring that operating cash flow was **negative $1.7 billion**; treating Adjusted EBITDA as owner cash without subtracting sustaining capital spending or interest; ignoring ATM dilution when sizing per-share owner cash.

**Inversion:** What would make this work? Sustained bitcoin above prior cycle highs, SEALMINER third-party sales at hardware margins, working capital release from inventory normalization, and contracted AI/colocation cash flow on **1.7 GW** of power footprint.
