## What this business is
Constellation Energy (NASDAQ: CEG) is the largest operator of nuclear power plants in the United States and one of the country's largest competitive power producers and retail electricity suppliers. **[Fact]** The fleet totals roughly **55 gigawatts** of capacity across nuclear, natural gas, geothermal, hydro, wind, and solar assets, enough to power the equivalent of **27 million homes**. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$25.5 billion** and normalized owner cash (operating cash flow minus capital spending) was **$1.29 billion**, or **$3.57 per share**. **[Fact]** At March 31, 2026 the company carried **$17.0 billion** of long-term debt and **$1.17 billion** of cash after closing the **Calpine** acquisition. **[Inference]** CEG sells reliable, emissions-free and dispatchable power into wholesale markets and through long-term contracts, with growing exposure to hyperscaler data-center demand.

---

## Why the market might be wrong
The market may be capitalizing **scarce nuclear capacity and AI-driven firm-power contracts** (including the Microsoft agreement to restart Crane Clean Energy Center) while underweighting **$17 billion of debt**, **capital spending that consumed 70% of operating cash flow in FY2025**, and **normalized owner cash of only $3.57 per share**. **[Inference]** At **$253.50**, the stock requires roughly **20%+ sustained owner-cash growth** and a mid-teens exit multiple just to break even on a Lawrence-style seven-year path, far above what FY2025 filings support. **[Inference]** Calpine adds scale and ERCOT exposure but also integration risk and incremental leverage that are not yet reflected in a clean owner-cash run-rate.

## Risks & inversion
**Primary risk:** **$17 billion** of debt and sustained capital spending keep normalized owner cash small relative to market capitalization, forcing equity holders to rely on AI/nuclear optionality and merchant power upside rather than near-term distributable cash.

**Additional risks:**

- **Calpine integration:** Goodwill and intangibles from acquisition; synergy realization uncertain.
- **Regulatory restart risk:** Crane Clean Energy Center timing subject to NRC and state approvals.
- **Merchant power exposure:** Revenue and earnings sensitive to wholesale power and capacity prices.
- **Rising rates:** Higher discount rates compress long-duration nuclear asset values.

**Lens failure mode (operating):** If normalized owner cash does not exceed **$6.00 per share** within three years while net debt remains above **$15 billion**, the AI-power scarcity narrative fails and the stock de-rates toward utility-like multiples on filing cash.
