## What this business is
The Cigna Group (NYSE: CI) is a large U.S. health services company organized in two reportable segments. **[Fact]** Evernorth Health Services provides pharmacy benefit management, specialty pharmacy, and care-delivery services to health plans, employers, and government programs. **[Fact]** Cigna Healthcare underwrites and administers commercial employer, Medicare Advantage, and international health coverage. **[Fact]** FY2025 consolidated revenue was **$274.9 billion** with **$9.6 billion** of cash from operations. **[Inference]** The equity is a scaled compounder on normalized owner cash: low capital intensity at the consolidated level, but medical-cost trend, Medicare rate cycles, and PBM regulation cap how much reinvestment converts to per-share growth.

## Why the market might be wrong
The market may be anchoring on **Medicare Advantage utilization noise** and **PBM legislative headlines** while underweighting **Evernorth's specialty-pharmacy mix**, **employer stickiness**, and **buyback-funded per-share compounding** at a price below our component base value. **[Inference]** At **$283.94**, the stock trades at a discount to a filing-based owner-earnings reinvestment model once long-term debt is taken from the FY2025 10-K rather than a stale 2018 tag; the gap is not a hidden-asset story but a **quality-at-a-discount** setup if medical trends normalize.

#### IRR arithmetic (show your work)

How we calculated the annual return (mechanical sync from `valuation.json` calculation proof):

1. Growth from reinvestment = reinvestment rate × incremental return on capital (base: 35% × 18% = 6.3% per year).
2. Year-one owner earnings = starting owner earnings × (1 + growth).
3. Distributable cash each year = owner earnings × (1 − reinvestment rate).
4. Present value of seven years of distributable cash at the discount rate.
5. Terminal value = year-seven owner earnings × terminal multiple; discounted to present.
6. Enterprise value = sum of present values.
7. Equity value = enterprise value + cash − long-term debt.
8. Value per share = equity value ÷ shares (**$502.23** base component value).
9. Annual return at today's price: **8.57% per year** at **$282.49** (contract: `valuation_contract.json`).
