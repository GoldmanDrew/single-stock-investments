## What this business is
Cummins Inc. (NYSE: CMI) designs, manufactures, and services diesel and natural-gas engines, generator sets, and related components for on-highway trucks, construction, mining, marine, and power-generation markets. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$33.67 billion** and operating income was **$4.03 billion**. **[Fact]** FY2025 operating cash flow was **$3.62 billion** and capital spending was **$1.24 billion**, leaving **$2.39 billion** of normalized owner earnings. **[Fact]** Reportable segments include Engine, Distribution, Components, Power Systems, and Accelera (zero-emissions technologies). **[Inference]** Cummins is a global industrial compounder with cyclical end markets, a large installed base that supports aftermarket revenue, and a strategic pivot toward hydrogen, battery, and fuel-cell products through Accelera.

---

## Why the market might be wrong
The market may be capitalizing **engine franchise durability and decarbonization optionality** while underweighting **$7.7 billion of debt** on the March 2026 balance sheet and **capital spending near $1.2 billion per year** that already reduces owner cash below reported operating cash flow. **[Inference]** At **$635.96**, the stock trades at roughly **2.3 times** our base proof value per share after refreshing debt from filings, implying the market expects reinvestment returns and terminal economics well above our bounded base case. Insider activity shows large net sales versus modest open-market buys over the past year, which weakens the "insiders know more" bull case unless those sales are largely planned 10b5-1 programs.

## Risks & inversion
**Primary risk:** Cyclical truck and construction downturn plus sustained Accelera investment could stall owner-earnings growth while **$7.7 billion** of debt limits financial flexibility.

**Additional risks:**

- **Emissions regulation:** Compliance costs and product transitions can compress margins if volumes weaken.
- **China exposure:** Local competition and demand swings affect Engine and Components segments.
- **Accelera execution:** Zero-emissions platforms may require years of spend before meaningful owner cash contribution.
- **Insider selling:** Net sales over the past year weaken insider-alignment signal despite strong operating results.

**Lens failure mode (operating):** If normalized owner earnings fall below **$1.8 billion** for two consecutive years while debt remains above **$7 billion**, the compounder narrative fails and the stock de-rates toward cyclical industrial multiples.
