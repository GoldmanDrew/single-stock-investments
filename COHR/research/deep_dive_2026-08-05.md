## What this business is
Coherent Corp. (NASDAQ: COHR) is a vertically integrated photonics and compound-semiconductor company formed when II-VI merged with Coherent Inc. in July 2022. **[Fact]** The company develops engineered materials, lasers, and optical components for datacom, industrial materials processing, aerospace and defense, and consumer electronics. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$5.81 billion** (+23% year over year), but net income fell to **$49 million** from **$156 million** as integration costs and amortization weighed on reported earnings. **[Fact]** Normalized owner cash (operating cash flow minus capital spending) was **$193 million**, or **$0.99 per share**, on **195.6 million** shares. **[Inference]** COHR sells critical optics and laser subsystems into AI datacenter build-out and telecom upgrade cycles, but heavy reinvestment and merger leverage keep distributable cash small relative to market capitalization.

## Risks & inversion
**Primary risk:** **$441 million** of annual capital spending and **$3.2 billion** of long-term debt keep normalized owner cash small relative to a **$55.8 billion** market capitalization, forcing equity holders to rely on AI photonics optionality rather than near-term distributable cash.

**Additional risks:**

- **Merger integration:** Goodwill and intangibles from the 2022 Coherent deal; FY2025 net income compression suggests costs not yet normalized.
- **Cyclical end markets:** Industrial laser and materials processing demand can mean-revert after expansion.
- **Customer concentration:** Hyperscaler and telecom OEM concentration creates pricing and order-timing risk.
- **Insider selling:** Large executive sales at elevated prices may signal limited near-term upside at current quotes.

**Lens failure mode (operating):** If normalized owner cash does not exceed **$2.00 per share** within three years while net debt remains above **$800 million**, the AI photonics scarcity narrative fails and the stock de-rates toward industrial-tech multiples on filing cash.
