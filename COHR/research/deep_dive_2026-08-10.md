## What this business is
Coherent Corp. (NYSE: COHR) designs and manufactures lasers, optical transceivers, engineered materials, and precision photonics systems for communications, industrial, electronics, and instrumentation markets. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$5.81 billion**, led by the Networking segment at **$3.42 billion** (+49% year over year). **[Fact]** FY2025 operating cash flow was **$634 million** and capital spending was **$441 million**, leaving **$193 million** of normalized owner earnings on a conservative operating-cash-flow-minus-capex basis. **[Fact]** The company carries about **$3.2 billion** of debt and **$2.2 billion** of cash on the March 2026 balance sheet after heavy datacom capacity investment. **[Inference]** Coherent is a capital-intensive photonics compounder riding AI datacenter optical demand while still digesting the II-VI merger, amortization, and preferred-dividend drag.

---

## Why the market might be wrong
The market may be capitalizing **AI datacenter transceiver and optical interconnect growth** while underweighting **$3.2 billion of debt**, **share dilution to 196 million shares**, and **owner cash that normalizes below $1 per share** after subtracting growth-heavy capital spending. **[Inference]** At **$379.13**, the stock trades near **25 times** our base proof value per share, implying reinvestment returns and terminal economics far above what FY2025 filings support. Insider activity shows large net sales versus modest open-market buys over the past year, which weakens the "insiders see more upside" narrative unless sales are largely planned programs.

## Risks & inversion
**Primary risk:** AI datacenter capex overshoot plus **$3.2 billion** of debt could leave normalized owner cash stagnant while the equity price embeds sustained hyper-growth.

**Additional risks:**

- **Customer concentration:** Hyperscaler and telecom OEM bargaining power can compress margins.
- **Goodwill impairment:** Lasers unit goodwill **~$3.2 billion**; auditor flagged impairment assessment.
- **Integration / amortization:** Merger-related intangible amortization weighs on reported earnings.
- **Share dilution:** Share count rose from **156M** to **196M** over two years.
- **Cyclical industrial exposure:** Materials and Lasers segments remain cycle-sensitive.

**Lens failure mode (operating):** If normalized owner earnings stay below **$150 million** for two consecutive fiscal years while debt remains above **$3 billion**, the compounder narrative fails and the stock de-rates toward cyclical industrial multiples.
