The issuer, CPCo, is dependent, in part, on the earnings of its subsidiaries.

Consequently, CPCo’s ability to service and to repay its debt when due, including the notes, may depend on the earnings of its subsidiaries, as well as its ability to receive funds from its subsidiaries through dividends or other payments or distributions. The ability of CPCo’s subsidiaries to pay dividends, repay intercompany debt or make other advances to it is subject to restrictions imposed by applicable laws (including bankruptcy laws), tax considerations and the terms of agreements governing its subsidiaries. CPCo’s foreign subsidiaries in particular may be subject to currency controls, repatriation restrictions, withholding obligations on payments to it, and other limits.

The notes are structurally subordinated to all of the indebtedness of CPCo’s subsidiaries.
The notes are CPCo’s general unsecured obligations and are not guaranteed by any of its subsidiaries.
