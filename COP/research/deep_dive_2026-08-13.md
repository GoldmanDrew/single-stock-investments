## What this business is

ConocoPhillips (NYSE: COP) is one of the largest independent exploration and production companies worldwide. **[Fact]** FY2025 total company production averaged **2,375 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day**, with operations across Alaska, the Lower 48 United States, Canada, Europe/Middle East/North Africa, and Asia Pacific. **[Fact]** FY2025 sales and other operating revenues were **$58.9 billion** and cash from operations **$19.8 billion**. **[Fact]** The company closed its **Marathon Oil** acquisition in 2024, expanding Lower 48 scale and reserve base. **[Inference]** Owner economics are commodity-linked but management emphasizes returns on capital, buybacks, and a variable dividend rather than volume-at-any-cost growth.

#### IRR arithmetic (show your work)

How we calculated the annual return (mechanical sync from `valuation.json` calculation proof after contract backfill):

1. Growth from reinvestment = reinvestment rate × incremental return on capital (base: 35% × 18% = 6.3% per year).
2. Year-one owner earnings = starting owner earnings × (1 + growth).
3. Distributable cash each year = owner earnings × (1 − reinvestment rate).
4. Present value of seven years of distributable cash at the discount rate.
5. Terminal value = year-seven owner earnings × terminal multiple; discounted to present.
6. Enterprise value = sum of present values.
7. Equity value = enterprise value + cash − total debt.
8. Value per share = equity value ÷ shares.
9. Annual return at today's price from `valuation_contract.json`.
