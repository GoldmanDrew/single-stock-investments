Item 8.01.

Other Events.

Appointment of J. David Joyner as Chair of the Board of Directors

On November 20, 2025, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of CVS Health Corporation (the “Company”) appointed J. David Joyner, President and Chief Executive Officer of the Company, to the additional role of Chair of the Board, effective January 1, 2026. Following the effective date of this appointment, Michael F. Mahoney will continue to serve as the Board’s Lead Independent Director and Roger N. Farah, who is currently serving as Executive Chair of the Board, will continue to serve on the Board.

Also on November 20, 2025, the Company issued a press release announcing the appointment of Mr. Joyner as Chair of the Board. A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
