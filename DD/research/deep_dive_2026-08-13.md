## What this business is
DuPont de Nemours, Inc. (NYSE: DD) is a specialty materials company focused on healthcare packaging, water filtration, electronics-adjacent polymers, and diversified industrial applications after a decade of portfolio reshaping. **[Fact]** FY2025 net sales were **$6.85 billion** and net income was **$779 million** on a recast two-segment structure: Healthcare & Water Technologies and Diversified Industrials. **[Fact]** The company completed the **Electronics** business separation in 2025, shrinking the balance sheet and debt stack versus pre-separation filings. **[Inference]** DD today is a mid-scale specialty compounder with cyclical industrial exposure, not the diversified chemical conglomerate implied by the legacy ticker history.

**Additional risks:**

- **Portfolio transition:** FY2025 segment realignment limits comparability; investors may mis-model run-rate margins.
- **Capex intensity:** **$333 million** FY2025 capex consumes most operating cash flow before reinvestment.
- **Industrial cyclicality:** Diversified Industrials exposes DD to auto and construction cycles.
- **Insider selling:** Net insider sales over the past year weaken alignment signal despite selective open-market buys.

**Lens failure mode (operating):** If normalized owner earnings fall below **$180 million** for two consecutive years while the share price remains above **$100**, the compounder narrative fails and the stock re-rates toward industrial mid-cycle multiples.
