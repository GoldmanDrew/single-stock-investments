Thank you for your message expressing support of Trian’s nomination of our CEO & Founding Partner, Nelson Peltz, and Jay Rasulo, to the Board of Directors of The Walt Disney Company.  We believe there is much that can be done to revive Disney, and we are excited about the opportunity to represent the interests of all shareholders in the boardroom.
