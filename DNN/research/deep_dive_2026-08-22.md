## What this business is
Denison Mines Corp. (NYSE American: DNN, TSX: DML) is a Canadian uranium exploration and development company focused on the Athabasca Basin in Saskatchewan. **[Fact]** The registrant filed Form 40-F for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 as an Ontario corporation listed on NYSE American and TSX. **[Fact]** At December 31, 2025 the company had **901,610,950** common shares outstanding and reported **no revenue from uranium mining** in FY2025 (revenue of CAD **4.9 million** related to contract-liability recognition, not production). **[Fact]** Total assets were CAD **1.11 billion**, including CAD **746 million** of financial assets marked at fair value (uranium prepayment and sale contracts per XBRL taxonomy members) and CAD **317 million** of property, plant and equipment. **[Inference]** The equity is primarily a call option on the Wheeler River project (Phoenix in-situ recovery deposit and Gryphon conventional deposit) plus a marked uranium inventory book and regional exploration interests, not a producing miner.

## Why the market might be wrong
The market may treat DNN as a levered uranium beta without separating **marked inventory**, **financing volatility**, and **permitting risk** on Phoenix. **[Fact]** FY2025 comprehensive loss was CAD **217.5 million**, but operating loss was CAD **61.4 million**; the remainder includes large fair-value and derivative charges tied to financing (ifrs-full: GainsLossesOnChangeInFairValueOfDerivatives CAD **111 million** loss). **[Inference]** Sentiment can overweight spot uranium moves while underpricing Saskatchewan provincial permitting timelines and the dilution path before first production. **[Inference]** Conversely, the market may under-appreciate the Phoenix feasibility completion (incorporated by reference in the 40-F, not yet in our downloaded exhibits) if investors still classify Denison as a speculative explorer rather than a permitted ISR candidate. Predictive attribute: **binary_milestone** (FID and permits on Phoenix).
