## What this business is
Healthpeak Properties (NYSE: DOC) is an S&P 500 real estate investment trust that owns healthcare properties across the United States: outpatient medical buildings, life-science lab campuses, and senior housing communities. **[Fact]** At December 31, 2025 the portfolio included interests in **689 properties** (507 outpatient medical, 145 lab, 34 senior housing) per `investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-K_20260203_rpt20251231_acc0001628280_26_005044.htm`. **[Fact]** FY2025 total revenue was **$2.82 billion** (up from **$2.70 billion** in FY2024) per `research/evidence/filing_facts_2026-08-06.json`. **[Fact]** FY2025 cash from operations was **$1.25 billion**; stockholders' equity was **$7.50 billion**; net income was **$71 million** (down from **$243 million** in FY2024). **[Fact]** The company is pursuing a **Janus Living** initial public offering to contribute its senior housing portfolio while retaining a majority stake and external management role.

## Why the market might be wrong
The stock may trade as a generic levered healthcare landlord after lab-tenant stress and merger integration noise, while the underlying property cash flows and strategic pivot (Janus spin, lab cluster additions) are more durable than headline GAAP earnings suggest. **[Inference]** FY2025 net income fell sharply even as revenue and operating cash flow grew, so generalists may anchor on **$0.10** basic earnings per share rather than **$1.25 billion** of operating cash flow against a **$10.4 billion** debt stack typical for a large REIT. **[Inference]** Proof-first component math at base case sums to about **−$3.89** per share of filing-locked economic value versus a price near **$22**, suggesting the market prices asset recovery, Janus Living optionality, and refinancing flexibility beyond what the conservative single-component reinvestment model captures today. **[Inference]** Outpatient medical and lab segments (roughly **$796 million** and **$567 million** of segment revenue in FY2025 per 10-K segment tables) may be undervalued relative to senior-housing execution risk that the Janus spin is meant to isolate.

## Executive summary
Healthpeak is a scaled U.S. healthcare REIT spanning outpatient medical, life-science lab, and senior housing assets, with heavy leverage and a planned Janus Living spin to simplify the story. FY2025 results show revenue growth and strong operating cash flow, but reported net income collapsed on non-cash and transaction items. The business is understandable as a levered real estate platform, but the bear case is not tightly bounded at the equity layer given debt maturity and lab-cycle exposure.
