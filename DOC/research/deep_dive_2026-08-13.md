## What this business is

Healthpeak Properties (NYSE: DOC) is an S&P 500 real estate investment trust that owns healthcare properties across the United States: outpatient medical buildings, life-science lab campuses, and senior housing communities. **[Fact]** At December 31, 2025 the portfolio included interests in **689 properties** (507 outpatient medical, 145 lab, 34 senior housing) per `investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-K_20260203_rpt20251231_acc0001628280_26_005044.htm`. **[Fact]** FY2025 total revenue was **$2.82 billion** (up from **$2.70 billion** in FY2024) per `research/evidence/filing_facts_2026-08-06.json`. **[Fact]** FY2025 cash from operations was **$1.25 billion**; stockholders' equity was **$7.50 billion**; net income was **$71 million** (down from **$243 million** in FY2024). **[Fact]** The company is pursuing a **Janus Living** initial public offering to contribute its senior housing portfolio while retaining a majority stake and external management role.
