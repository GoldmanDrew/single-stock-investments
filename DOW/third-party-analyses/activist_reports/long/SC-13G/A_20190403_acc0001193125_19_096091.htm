ITEM 5

OWNERSHIP OF FIVE PERCENT OR LESS OF A CLASS:

If this statement is being filed to report the fact that, as of the date hereof, the reporting person has ceased to be the beneficial owner of more than 5 percent of the class of securities, check the following:     ☒

On April 1, 2019, DowDuPont completed the previously announced separation of its materials science business by way of a pro rata distribution of all of the then-issued and outstanding shares of common stock of Dow to DowDuPont’s stockholders. As a result of certain internal reorganization transactions undertaken by DowDuPont in connection with the separation, TDCC became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Dow, and, as a result of the distribution, Dow became an independent publicly traded company and DowDuPont ceased to have any beneficial ownership of Dow. As a result, DowDuPont ceased to beneficially own any shares of Common Stock.
