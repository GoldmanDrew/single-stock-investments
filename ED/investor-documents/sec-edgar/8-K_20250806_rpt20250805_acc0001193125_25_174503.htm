Item 5.02
Departure of Directors or Principal Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Principal Officers
On August 5, 2025, the Board of Directors of Consolidated Edison, Inc. (“Con Edison”) and the Board of Trustees of Consolidated Edison Company of New York, Inc. (collectively, the “Boards”) each elected Brendan Cavanagh as a member of the Boards, effective October 1, 2025. Mr. Cavanagh was appointed to the Safety, Environment, Operations and Sustainability Committees and the Audit Committees of the Boards, effective October 1, 2025. Mr. Cavanagh will participate in the compensation arrangements described under “Director Compensation” in Con Edison’s proxy statement for its 2025 annual meeting of stockholders (filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on April 9, 2025). Mr. Cavanagh has served as President and Chief Executive Officer of SBA Communications Corporation since January 2024.
