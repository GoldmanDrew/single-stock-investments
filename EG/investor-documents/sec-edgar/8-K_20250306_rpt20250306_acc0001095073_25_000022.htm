ITEM 5.02    Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

On March 6, 2025, the Board of Directors (the "Board") of Everest Group, Ltd. (the "Company") increased its size to ten members and elected John Howard to serve on the Board, effective immediately. Mr. Howard was appointed to serve as a member of Board's Audit Committee, Compensation Committee and Nominating & Governance Committee, effective immediately. He will receive an annual retainer of $125,000, pro-rated based on the date of his election to the Board and an annual grant of restricted shares with a fair market value of $325,000 pursuant to the 2003 Non-Employee Director Compensation Plan. A press release announcing Mr. Howard's appointment is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1.
