ITEM 7.01    REGULATION FD DISCLOSURE
On April 13, 2026, Everest Group, Ltd. (the “Company”) published a recast quarterly financial supplement for the fourth quarter of 2025 on the Company website, which contains prior period data for the first quarter of 2024 through the fourth quarter of 2025. Effective January 1, 2026, Everest Group (the "Company") changed its reportable segments to: Reinsurance Treaty, Global Wholesale and Specialty, and Legacy, following the sale of the renewal rights for its Commercial Retail Insurance business in the U.S. and certain regions globally to American International Group, Inc. Accordingly, the Company revised the presentation of the data in the financial supplement to reflect the new business segments. The recast financial supplement is unaudited.
The fourth quarter 2025 financial supplement recast will be posted to the Company website in this revised format.

The above information is being furnished under Item 7.01, “Regulation FD Disclosure.” This information shall not be deemed filed for the purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section.
