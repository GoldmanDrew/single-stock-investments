The shares of Common Stock that may be deemed to be beneficially owned by the Reporting Person, as reported herein, were acquired for investment purposes. The Reporting Person retains the right to change its investment intent, from time to time, and to engage in transactions to acquire, sell, hedge, pledge, or use as collateral for one or more loans or credit facilities the shares of Common Stock or other securities of the Issuer owned by it, based on market conditions and other factors it may deem relevant. However, as part of the ongoing evaluation of the investment and investment alternatives, the Reporting Person may consider such matters and, subject to applicable law, may formulate a plan with respect to such matters, and, from time to time, may hold discussions with or make formal proposals to management or the Board of Directors of the Issuer or other third parties regarding such matters.
