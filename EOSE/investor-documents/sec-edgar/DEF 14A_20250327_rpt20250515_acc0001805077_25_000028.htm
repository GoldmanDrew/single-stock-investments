Milestone PRSUs
In June 2024, the Company entered into a credit and guaranty agreement with CCM Denali Debt Holdings, LP and related securities purchase agreement with CCM Denali Equity Holdings, LP (collectively, CCM Denali Debt Holdings, LP and CCM Denali Equity Holdings, LP are referred to as “Cerberus,” and the agreements, the “Cerberus Agreements”). The borrowings under the Cerberus Agreements were conditioned on the Company achieving certain performance-based milestones related to the Company’s automated line, materials cost, Z3 technology and backlog/cash conversion. Upon any failure to achieve a milestone, in addition to not being able to require a draw pursuant to the Cerberus Agreements on the first three measurement dates, the Company would be obligated to issue to Cerberus certain warrants and/or preferred stock, convertible into up to 4% of fully diluted common shares for all performance milestone on each milestone measurement (or a maximum of 16% in the aggregate) (such additional issuances, the “Equity Penalty”).
In recognition of both: (i) the dilutive impact that the Equity Penalty would have on our stockholders, and (ii) the negative impact of the potential loss of access to the funding under the Cerberus Agreements on our ability to continue to operate and build shareholder value, the Compensation Committee determined it was critical to the success and value of the Company to directly align the interests of our key employees – including the NEOs – with achievement of the performance milestones set forth under the Cerberus Agreements. As a result, the Compensation Committee determined that 25% of the NEOs’ 2024 annual equity awards should be in the form of Milestone PRSUs. In addition, the Compensation Committee also approved annual equity grants to all exempt employees, consisting of 50% Milestone PRSUs and 50% RSUs. The Compensation Committee determined the number of Milestone PRSUs to be awarded upon the achievement of the performance milestones such that the number of securities issuable under the Milestone PRSUs, and therefore the dilution to our common stockholders, would be substantially less than the securities that would have been issuable to Cerberus, and accordingly the dilution to common stockholders related thereto, upon the failure to achieve any such Equity Penalty.

The following discussion summarizes certain material federal income tax consequences of the issuance and receipt of awards under the Amended 2020 Plan under the law as in effect on the date of this proxy statement. •Incentive Stock Options (ISOs). An optionee generally realizes no taxable income upon the grant or exercise of an ISO. However, the exercise of an ISO may result in an alternative minimum tax liability to the employee. With some exceptions, a disposition of shares purchased under an ISO within two years from the date of grant or within one year after exercise produces ordinary income to the optionee equal to the value of the shares at the time of exercise less the exercise price. The same amount is deductible by the Company as compensation, provided that the Company reports the income to the optionee. If a sale or disposition of shares acquired with the ISO occurs after the holding period, the employee will recognize long-term capital gain or loss at the time of sale equal to the difference between proceeds realized and the exercise price paid. In general, an ISO that is exercised by the optionee more than three months after termination of employment is treated as an NQSO.
