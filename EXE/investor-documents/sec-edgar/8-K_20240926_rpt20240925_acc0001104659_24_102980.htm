Item 8.01Other Events

 

On September 26, 2024, Chesapeake Energy Corporation, an Oklahoma corporation (“Chesapeake”), and Southwestern Energy Company, a Delaware corporation (“Southwestern”), issued a joint press release announcing that the waiting period in connection with the companies’ pending combination under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976 has expired.
