OCG, through Oaktree Capital Holdings, LLC, is the indirect manager of each of XI CHK Holdings, Xb CHK Holdings, Xb Holdings, and Xi Holdings. OCGH GP is the indirect owner of the class B units of OCG. BAM is the indirect owner of the class A units of OCG. BAM Partnership is the sole owner of the Class B Limited Voting Shares of BAM. As a result of the foregoing relationships, OCG, OCGH GP, BAM, and BAM Partnership may be deemed beneficial owners of the securities held directly by XI CHK Holdings, Xb CHK Holdings, Xb Holdings, and Xi Holdings.
