Item 8.01.Other Events.

  

On April 13, 2026, Diamondback Energy, Inc. (“Diamondback”) announced the final tender results of its previously announced tender offers (the “Offers”) to purchase for cash any and all of Diamondback’s outstanding 4.400% Senior Notes due 2051 (the “2051 Notes”) and 4.250% Senior Notes due 2052 (together with the 2051 Notes, the “Notes”) from holders of each series of the Notes. The Company issued (i) a press release announcing the pricing of the Offers on April 10, 2026 and (ii) a press release announcing the results of the Offers on April 13, 2026. Copies of these press releases are attached as Exhibits 99.1 and 99.2, respectively, hereto and are incorporated by reference herein.
