Item 8.01. Other Events

Redemption of Subordinated Bank Notes. On January 13, 2026 Fifth Third Bancorp (the “Company”) announced that the Company’s subsidiary, Fifth Third Bank, National Association (the “Bank”), delivered a redemption notice to The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A., as issuing and paying agent (the “Agent”), to redeem on February 13, 2026 (the “Redemption Date”) all of the Bank’s outstanding 3.850% Subordinated Notes due March 15, 2026 (CUSIP No. 31677AAB0), originally issued on March 15, 2016 (the “Subordinated Bank Notes”), at a cash redemption price (the “Redemption Price”) to be calculated as provided in the Subordinated Bank Notes, equal to the $750 million principal amount of the outstanding Subordinated Bank Notes plus accrued and unpaid interest, if any, to but excluding the Redemption Date.



Pursuant to the terms and conditions of the Subordinated Bank Notes, the Company will redeem the Subordinated Bank Notes prior to their scheduled maturity date of March 15, 2026 pursuant to the initial optional redemption provisions contained therein. Upon completion of the redemptions, no Subordinated Bank Notes will remain outstanding.

On or after the Redemption Date, payment of the Redemption Price will be made only upon presentation and surrender to the Agent of the Subordinated Bank Notes. Interest on the Subordinated Bank Notes called for redemption will cease to accrue on and after the Redemption Date. Notice of redemption will be sent by the Agent to the registered holders of the Subordinated Bank Notes

A copy of the press release announcing the notice of the redemption is attached to this Current Report on Form 8-K as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
