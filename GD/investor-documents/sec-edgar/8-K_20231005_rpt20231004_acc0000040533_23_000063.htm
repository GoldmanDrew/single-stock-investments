Item 5.03    Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year.

On October 4, 2023, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of General Dynamics Corporation (the “Corporation”) amended and restated Article III, Section 2 of the Corporation’s bylaws (the “Bylaws”), effective as of such date, to:
(i)increase the director retirement age, subject to certain exceptions set forth in the Bylaws; and
(ii)increase the age beyond which an individual may not stand for election to the Board.

The foregoing summary is qualified in its entirety by reference to the text of the Bylaws of the Corporation, filed herewith as Exhibit 3.2 and incorporated herein by reference.
