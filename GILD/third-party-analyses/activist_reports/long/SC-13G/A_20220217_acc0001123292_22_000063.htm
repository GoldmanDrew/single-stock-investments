Explanatory Note

This Amendment No. 1 (the “Amendment”) amends the Schedule 13G filed by Gilead Sciences, Inc. on January 28, 2019. The Amendment was timely filed on February 10, 2022, but was improperly filed under EDGAR Form Type 13G (as an original Schedule 13G filing) instead of under Form Type 13G/A (as an amendment to Schedule 13G). The Amendment is hereby refiled in order to correctly reflect that the Amendment is not an original Schedule 13G. The information contained in this Amendment is unchanged from that filed on February 10, with the exception of this Explanatory Note.
