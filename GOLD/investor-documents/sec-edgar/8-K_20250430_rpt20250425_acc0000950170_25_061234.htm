Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

On April 25, 2025, five recently acquired direct and indirect subsidiaries (the “Subsidiaries”) of A-Mark Precious Metals, Inc. (the “Company) entered into a Joinder to Guaranty and Collateral Agreement, dated as of April 18, 2025 (the "Joinder") with CIBC Bank USA as administrative agent for the Company's lenders.

The Joinder makes each of the Subsidiaries a Guarantor of and a Loan Party to the Company’s Credit Agreement, dated December 21, 2021, as amended by amendments First through Twelfth (the “Credit Agreement”), which provides for a revolving credit facility.

This description is qualified by reference to the text of the Joinder, which will be filed as an exhibit to the Company’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ending June 30, 2025.
