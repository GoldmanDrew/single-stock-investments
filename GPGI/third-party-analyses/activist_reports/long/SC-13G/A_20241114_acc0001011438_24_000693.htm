LMR Multi-Strategy Master Fund Limited (“LMR Master Fund”) directly holds warrants to purchase 2,642,329 shares of Class A Common Stock and LMR CCSA Master Fund Ltd (“LMR CCSA Master Fund”) directly holds warrants to purchase 2,642,330 shares of Class A Common Stock.
