Item 8.01

Other Events.

On September 8, 2023, Warrior Met Coal, Inc. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing the expiration and results of its previously announced (i) offer to purchase (the “Restricted Payment Offer”), in cash, up to $150,000,000 principal amount of the Company’s outstanding 7.875% Senior Secured Notes due 2028 (the “Notes”), upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Restricted Payment Notice and Offer to Purchase, dated August 9, 2023, and (ii) cash tender offer (the “Tender Offer” and, together with the Restricted Payment Offer, the “Offers”) to purchase up to $150,000,000 principal amount of the Notes upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase, dated August 9, 2023. The Offers expired at 5:00 P.M. (New York City time) on September 7, 2023.

A copy of the press release announcing the expiration and results of the Offers is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated by reference into this Item 8.01.
