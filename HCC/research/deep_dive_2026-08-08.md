## What this business is
Warrior Met Coal is a pure-play producer of premium steelmaking (metallurgical) coal from underground longwall mines in Alabama. **[Fact]** The company operates Mine No. 4, Mine No. 7, and the developing Blue Creek project, and produced **9.3 million metric tons** of steelmaking coal in 2025. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$1.31 billion**, operating income **$46 million**, net income **$57 million**, and operating cash flow **$229 million** per `HCC/investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-K_20260212_rpt20251231_acc0001193125_26_048914.htm`. **[Fact]** At June 30, 2026 the balance sheet showed **$310 million** of cash (including restricted), **$155 million** of long-term debt, and **$2.29 billion** of stockholders' equity on **52.8 million** shares outstanding per `HCC/investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-Q_20260805_rpt20260630_acc0001193125_26_335324.htm`.

---

## Why the market might be wrong
The stock may be pricing a full met coal up-cycle while our normalized component base values trough-to-midcycle owner economics closer to **$12 per share** versus a price near **$90**. **[Inference]** Q2 2026 results were strong (revenue **$504 million**, net income **$87 million**, diluted earnings **$1.65** per share), and generalists may extrapolate that quarter into a sustained peak. **[Inference]** The market may also credit Blue Creek development and net cash without applying an explicit supply-response reserve when seaborne met coal prices normalize. **[Inference]** Conversely, if low-cost longwall position and net cash survive a prolonged trough better than smaller peers, the bear case on absolute bankruptcy risk may be too harsh; dhando is partial, not full.

---

## Executive summary
Warrior Met Coal is a low-leverage Alabama met coal producer rebounding from a 2025 trough. At **$89.61**, we expect about **2.29%** per year over seven years on normalized owner cash. Component proofs sum to roughly **$12** per share versus price near **$90**, so the market may be capitalizing cycle recovery. We propose **watch**.

## Risks & inversion

**Inversion checklist:**
- If H2 2026 operating cash flow annualizes below **$200 million**, trough normalization was too optimistic.
- If long-term debt rises above **$300 million** while owner cash stays below **$250 million**, balance-sheet advantage erodes.
- If Blue Creek capex overruns delay second-longwall production beyond filed guidance, remove option value from base case.

**Lens failure mode (capital cycle):** Capitalizing spot or near-spot margins after a trough rebound, the classic peak-margin failure mode for the capital_cycle profile.
