Item 9.01 Financial Statements and Exhibits
On April 30, 2024, The Hartford Financial Services Group, Inc. (the “Company”), through Hartford Accident and Indemnity Company, an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, made a donation of 250,000 shares of its common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the “Common Stock”), to HFPG, Inc., an affiliate of Hartford Foundation for Public Giving, in furtherance of the Company’s philanthropic goals. As a result, the Company has registered 250,000 shares of its Common Stock with the United States Securities and Exchange Commission under its Registration Statement on Form S-3 (No. 333-262879) for purposes of potential resale by HFPG, Inc., and is filing hereby certain related exhibits.
