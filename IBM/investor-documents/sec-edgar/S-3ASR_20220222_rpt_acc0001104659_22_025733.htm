About International Business Machines Corporation

(C-T-R), a consolidation of the Computing Scale Co. of America, the Tabulating Machine Co. and The International Time Recording Co. of New York. This was signaled almost 100 years ago, in 1924, when C-T-R changed its name to International Business Machines Corporation.
