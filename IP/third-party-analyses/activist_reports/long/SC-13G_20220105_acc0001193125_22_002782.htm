Note 1: On October 1, 2021, International Paper Company (“International Paper”) completed the spin-off of Sylvamo Corporation (the “Issuer”) through the distribution of approximately 80.1% of the shares of common stock of the Issuer to International Paper’s shareholders. Pursuant to the Registration Rights Agreement, dated as of September 30, 2021, between International Paper and the Issuer, International Paper granted to the Issuer a proxy to vote the shares of the Issuer’s common stock owned by International Paper in proportion to the votes cast by the Issuer’s other stockholders.
