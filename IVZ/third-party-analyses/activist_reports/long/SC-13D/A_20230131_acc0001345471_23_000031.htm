This Amendment No. 7 amends and supplements the Schedule 13D filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on September 22, 2020 (the “Original Schedule 13D”), as amended by Amendment No. 1 filed on November 5, 2020, Amendment No. 2 filed on December 15, 2021, Amendment No. 3 filed on February 1, 2022, Amendment No. 4 filed on March 25, 2022, Amendment No. 5 filed on April 1, 2022, and as amended by Amendment No. 6 (“Amendment No. 6”) filed on January 25, 2023 (as amended, the “Schedule 13D”), relating to the Common Stock, par value $0.20 per share (the “Shares”), of Invesco Ltd., a Bermuda exempted company (the “Issuer”). The address of the principal executive office of the Issuer is 1555 Peachtree Street, NE., Suite 1800, Atlanta, Georgia 30309.
 
Capitalized terms not defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Schedule 13D.  Except as set forth herein, the Schedule 13D is unmodified.
 
Items 4 and Item 5 of the Schedule 13D are hereby amended and supplemented as follows:

Item 4. Purpose of the Transaction

Item 4 of the Schedule 13D is hereby amended and supplemented by adding the following information:

The sales of Shares by Reporting Persons reported below in Item 5 were done for portfolio management purposes.

Item 5. Interest in Securities of the Issuer

Item 5 of the Schedule 13D is hereby amended and supplemented by adding the following information:

(a) As of 4:00 p.m., New York City time, on January 31, 2023, the Reporting Persons beneficially owned, in the aggregate, 40,758,634 Shares, representing approximately 8.96% of the Issuer’s outstanding Shares (calculated based on 454,784,608 Shares outstanding as of September 30, 2022, as reported in the Issuer’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2022).

(b) Trian AM Holdco beneficially and directly owns and has sole voting power and sole dispositive power with regard to 40,745,000 Shares, except to the extent that other Reporting Persons as described in this Item 5(b) may be deemed to have shared voting power and shared dispositive power with regard to such Shares.

(c)   Set forth below is a list of all transactions with respect to the Shares effected by any of the Reporting Persons since the filing of Amendment No. 6, inclusive of all transactions effected through 4:00 pm, New York City time, on January 31, 2023.  All such transactions listed in the table below were effected in the open market or through block trades.  The prices set forth in the table do not include commissions.
