Item 8.01. Other Events.

 

On August 15, 2025, U.S. Bank Trust Company, N.A. (“US Bank”) was appointed as trustee, paying agent and registrar under Keurig Dr Pepper Inc.’s (the “Company”) indenture, dated as of December 15, 2009, including with respect to each supplemental indenture thereto, and the Company’s indenture, dated as of May 25, 2018, including with respect to each supplemental indenture thereto. (“Computershare”) as the trustee, paying agent and registrar for such indentures, with Computershare having resigned as the trustee, paying agent and registrar under such indentures. The change with respect to the paying agent and registrar is expected to become effective August 25, 2025.
