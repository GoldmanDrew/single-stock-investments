Item 7.01

Regulation FD Disclosure

On March 5, 2025, Lithium Americas Corp. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing that the Company has secured a commitment for a strategic investment of $250 million from Orion Resource Partners LP. A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

On March 5, 2025, the Company also made available an investor presentation providing highlights of the investment. The investor presentation can be found on the Company’s website at www.lithiumamericas.com.
