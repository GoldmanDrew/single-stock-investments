Item 5(a) is amended and restated to read in its entirety as follows:

Item 6.

Contracts, Arrangements, Understandings or Relationships with Respect to Securities of the Issuer.

Item 6 of the Schedule 13D is hereby amended and supplemented as follows:

On April 25, 2024, LIPO Investments (USA) Inc. (the “Borrower”) entered into a margin loan agreement with Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC (the “Lender”) pursuant to which the Borrower may borrow up to $200 million. Upon the occurrence of certain events that are customary for this type of loan, the Lender may exercise its rights to require the Borrower to pre-pay the loan proceeds, post additional collateral, or foreclose on, and dispose of, the shares held as collateral. As of the date of this filing, a total of 1,830,000 shares of lululemon athletica inc. are held in one or more margin accounts subject to a standard margin loan arrangement.
