Item 4. Ownership.

 

Provide the following information regarding the aggregate number and percentage of the class of securities of issuer identified in Item 1.

 

  (a)

Amount beneficially owned:

 

As of December 31, 2018, Anderson directly owned 41,446,985 Class A Ordinary Shares in the form of the Issuer’s American depositary shares. Anderson is wholly-owned by Thomson, which in turn is wholly-owned by Tembusu. Tembusu is wholly-owned by Temasek Holdings. Accordingly, as of the date of December 31, 2018, each of Temasek Holdings, Tembusu and Thomson may be deemed to beneficially own the 41,446,985 Class A Ordinary Shares held by Anderson.
