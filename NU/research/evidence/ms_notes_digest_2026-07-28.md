## 3. Competition (Mercado Pago / Sea / others)

**Mercado Pago / MELI (MS Rise, “Implications for Mercado Pago”):**

- Traction in *usage* is real (17% of Brazil consumers have some banking relationship).  
- *Principality* is earlier-stage (5% primary; 4% salary deposits).  
- Strengths: personal loans, e-commerce tie-ins, card intent (esp. higher-income cohorts), marketplace data / promotions.  
- MS frames cards as MELI moving upmarket; still complementary ecosystem credit, not yet Nu-like primary bank.

**PicPay:** 18% any / 3% primary in same survey; younger / lower-income skew.

**Sea / Shopee / Monee:** **No material discussion** in these five PDFs (same absence pattern as NU 20-F).

**Incumbents:** Share donors to Nu since 2021 survey mainly Caixa, Banco do Brasil, Bradesco; Itaú defended better.

## 4. US expansion (MS)

**Opportunity:** Fragmented retail deposits (~US$7.4Tn retail deposit pool in note); pay-up for deposits works for digital peers; Latino / cross-border / remittances adjacency; cost advantage vs US banks.

**Challenges:** Fully banked market; high CAC (US$200–1,000+ vs ~US$7 LatAm in note); primary-account switching friction (payroll/bills); rewards-heavy card competition; risk of management distraction / cash burn. MS: large but **selective**; deposits first, cards harder.

## 5. Growth levers / $100B path

- Brazil still core; Mexico becoming second leg (breakeven, license unlock).  
- AI: underwriting + deposit-yield optimization + personalization.  
- US: capped-cost test.  
- Path note: ARPAC expansion + multi-country ROE compounding (model assumptions older; treat as directional).
