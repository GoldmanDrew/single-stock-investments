Explanatory Note

 

This Amendment No. 1 to Schedule 13D (this “Amendment No. 1”) amends and supplements the statement on Schedule 13D filed with the United States Securities and Exchange Commission on May 16, 2024 (as amended to date, the “Schedule 13D”), relating to the Class A common stock, par value $0.0001 per share (the “ Class A Common Stock”), of Oklo Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Issuer”). This Amendment No. 1 amends and restates the ownership numbers previously reported in the Schedule 13D, which inadvertently omitted shares held by the Reporting Persons’ various family members. Capitalized terms used herein without definition shall have the meaning set forth in the Schedule 13D.

 

Item 5.Interest in Securities of the Issuer.

 

Item 5 of the Schedule 13D is hereby amended and restated in its entirety as follows:

 

(a)– (b)
