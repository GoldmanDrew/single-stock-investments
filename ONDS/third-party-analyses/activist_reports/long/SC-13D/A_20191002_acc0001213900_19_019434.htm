Item 1.Security and Issuer

 

The class of equity securities to which this Schedule 13D relates is the common stock, $0.0001 par value, of Ondas Holdings Inc., a Nevada corporation (the “Company” or “Issuer”). The address of the principal executive office of the Company is 165 Gibraltar Court, Sunnyvale, CA 94089.

 

Item 2.Identity and Background.

 

(a) Name: Eric A. Brock (the “Reporting Person”).

 

(b) Residence or Business Address of Reporting Person:

 

165 Gibraltar Court, Sunnyvale, CA 94089.

 

(c) Present principal occupation or employment of Reporting Person: Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Ondas Holdings Inc.

 

(d) During the last five years, the Reporting Person has not been convicted in a criminal proceeding (excluding traffic violations or similar misdemeanors).

 

(e) During the last five years, the Reporting Person has not been a party to a civil proceeding of a judicial or administrative body of competent jurisdiction which as a result of such proceeding was or is subject to a judgment, decree or final order enjoining future violations of, or prohibiting or mandating activities subject to, federal or state securities laws or finding violation with respect to such laws.

 

(f) Citizenship: Mr. Brock is a citizen of the United States of America.

 

Item 3.Source and Amount of Funds or Other Consideration.

 

PF-Personal Funds

 

Acquisition of Shares of Ondas Holdings Inc.

 

On May 22, 2018, the sole officer, director and majority shareholder of the Issuer, formerly known as Zev Ventures Incorporated, sold 500,000 shares of common stock to the Reporting Person for $0.124 per share, or an aggregate purchase price of $64,000.

 

On August 13, 2018, the Issuer affected a forward stock split of its outstanding shares of common stock on a 10 for 1 basis. On the pay date of August 14, 2018, the Reporting Person was issued shares of common stock in payment of the forward stock split resulting in an adjusted ownership of 5,000,000 shares of common stock of the Issuer.

 

On September 27, 2019, the Reporting Person entered into a Securities Purchase Agreement with other subscribers in which he purchased 400,000 units (wherein a unit consisted of one share of Company common stock and one-half of one warrant to purchase one share of Company common stock (the “Investor Warrant”)) of the Issuer for $1,000,000. The Investor Warrant vests six months from the date of the Securities Purchase Agreement and may be exercised at a price of $3.25 per share. The Reporting Person’s ownership does not include shares underlying the Investor Warrant.

Purchase of Shares of Ondas Networks Inc., a private company

 

On June 20, 2017, the Reporting Person entered into a Subscription Agreement and loaned Ondas Networks, Inc. (“Ondas Networks”), formerly known as Full Spectrum Inc., the amount of $100,000 under a Convertible Promissory Note. The amount due under the Convertible Promissory Note was subsequently converted into 19,248 shares of common stock of Ondas Networks.

 

Exchange of Ondas Networks’ Shares for Ondas Holdings’ Shares

 

On September 28, 2018, Ondas Networks was acquired as a wholly owned subsidiary by the Issuer in which the 19,248 shares of Ondas Networks owned by the Reporting Person were exchanged for shares of the Issuer at an exchange rate of one share of Ondas Networks for 3.823 shares of the Issuer, wherein the Reporting Person received 73,585 shares of common stock of the Issuer.

 

Mr. Brock currently owns 5,473,585 shares of common stock of the Issuer.
