There was no Class B ordinary share outstanding as of March 31, 2021 as all Class B ordinary shares were converted into Class A ordinary shares in March 2021. Each Class A ordinary share is entitled to one vote. Accordingly, the Class A ordinary shares beneficially owned by Banyan Fund II represent approximately 6.36% of the aggregate voting power of the total issued and outstanding ordinary shares of the Issuer.
