Item 8.01 – Other Events.

On January 21, 2025, Riot Platforms, Inc. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing an update on strategic actions it is taking to maximize the value of its power asset at its facility in Navarro County, Texas (the “Corsicana Facility”). The Company is evaluating the feasibility of developing its remaining 600 megawatts of power capacity, at the Corsicana Facility, for artificial intelligence (“AI”)/high-performance computing (“HPC”) uses. In connection with its evaluation of such opportunities, the Company has halted the development of its Phase II Bitcoin mining expansion at the Corsicana Facility.

The foregoing summary of the press release is not intended to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the press release, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
