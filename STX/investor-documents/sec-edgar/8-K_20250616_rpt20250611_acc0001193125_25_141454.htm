Item 1.02. Termination of a Material Definitive Agreement.

On June 11, 2025 (the “Redemption Date”), the Seagate HDD Cayman (“Seagate HDD”), a direct subsidiary of Seagate Technology Holdings public limited company (the “Company”), redeemed all of its remaining outstanding 4.875% Senior Notes due 2027 (the “2027 Notes”), which were issued under that certain Indenture, dated as of May 14, 2015, by and among Seagate HDD, each of the guarantors party thereto and Computershare Trust Company, National Association (as successor to Wells Fargo Bank, National Association), as trustee (as amended and supplemented, including by that certain Supplemental Indenture, dated as of May 18, 2021, the “Indenture”), at a redemption price of 100.819% of the outstanding aggregate principal amount of the Notes, plus accrued and unpaid interest thereon to, but excluding, the Redemption Date. Following the redemption of the 2027 Notes, Seagate HDD satisfied and discharged all obligations under the Indenture.
