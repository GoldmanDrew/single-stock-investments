Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. 

 

On November 10, 2024, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of The Bancorp, Inc. (the “Company”) and its wholly-owned subsidiary, The Bancorp Bank, National Association, received notification from director Daniela A. Mielke of her resignation from the Board, effective immediately, thereby terminating her directorship as of that date. Ms. Mielke voluntarily resigned to pursue other business opportunities, and her resignation was not the result of any disagreement with the Company, the Company’s management, or the Board or any committee thereof, on any matter relating to the Company’s operations, policies, or practices, or otherwise. The Company thanks Ms. Mielke for her years of service and leadership.

 

In light of Ms. Mielke’s resignation from the Board, the Board has reduced its size from ten (10) directors to nine (9) directors, effective November 12, 2024.
