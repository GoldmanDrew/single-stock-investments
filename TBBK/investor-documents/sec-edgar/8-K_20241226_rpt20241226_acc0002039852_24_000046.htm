Item 8.01. Other Events

 

The Bancorp, Inc. (the “Company”) is providing an update on the disposition of certain real estate bridge lending  assets.

As noted in its prior filings, the majority of the Company’s real estate owned is comprised of an apartment complex, with a balance as of December 19, 2024 of $41.1 million. That property is under agreement of sale with a sales price that is expected to cover the Company’s current balance plus the forecasted cost of improvements to the property. The purchaser has increased the total of earnest money deposits to $1.6 million, from $500,000, in consideration of extending the  closing date to March 21, 2025. The Company believes that the purpose for the extension is to allow time for this sale to be included  in a larger transaction. There can be no assurance that the purchaser will consummate the sale of the property, but if not consummated,  the earnest money deposits of $1.6 million would accrue to the Company.

The Company is in discussions with multiple parties to sell other loans, including balances reflected in classified loans.
