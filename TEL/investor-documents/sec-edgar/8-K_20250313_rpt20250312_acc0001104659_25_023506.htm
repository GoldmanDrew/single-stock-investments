Item 5.07.  Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

 

At the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders of TE Connectivity plc (the “Company”) held on March 12, 2025 (the “AGM”), shareholders voted on the agenda items described below.

 

Proxies for the AGM were solicited pursuant to Regulation 14A under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, and there was no solicitation in opposition to the Company’s solicitation.

 

A total of 271,942,700 ordinary shares (91.1% of 298,472,685 ordinary shares outstanding and entitled to vote as of January 13, 2025, the record date for the AGM) were present by proxy, constituted a quorum for the transaction of business, and were voted at the AGM. The agenda items submitted at the AGM were passed as described below. Percentages indicated below reflect the percentage of the total number of votes cast with respect to the proposal.
