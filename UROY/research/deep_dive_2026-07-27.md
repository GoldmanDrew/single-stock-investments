## What this business is
New URC bolts **Sweetwater Royalties** (perpetual Wyoming Green River trona / soda ash royalties plus a huge fee land estate) onto **Uranium Royalty Corp.** (uranium royalties, streams, and residual physical uranium). Sweetwater’s operators (WE Soda, Tata, Sisecam, Solvay/American Soda) have mined the basin for about 50 years. URC brings uranium deal skills and a mostly pre-cash royalty book. The public parent moves to Delaware / NASDAQ and exits the Canadian PFIC profile for *new* U.S. holders after domestication.

---

## Why the market might be wrong
The market may still price this as a sleepy foreign uranium royalty name, or as a soda-ash story at the wrong point in the cycle. The real security is a **levered scarce-land royalty stub**: cash today is weak because soda ash is in a deep downturn, but the mineral estate is long-duration and capex-light for the owner. Mispricing can also come from **deal optics** (deemed US$3.64) versus **equity math after US$625m debt**.

---

## Executive summary
At a US$2.96 listing proxy (today’s UROY print on a 1-for-1 exchange into New URC), the base case owner path is about **−3% per year** over seven years if midcycle cash only partially recovers and options stay half-recognized. That is **not** an accumulate signal on cash alone. The bull path (capacity growth + cycle + uranium/land options) can still clear a high-teens return, which is why Power Zone personas disagree.
