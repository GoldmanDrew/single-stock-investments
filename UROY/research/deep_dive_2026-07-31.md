## What this business is

New URC bolts **Sweetwater Royalties** (perpetual Wyoming Green River trona and soda ash royalties plus a large fee land estate) onto **Uranium Royalty Corp.** (uranium royalties, streams, and residual physical uranium). **[Fact]** Sweetwater's operators (WE Soda, Tata, Sisecam, Solvay/American Soda) have mined the basin for about 50 years; New URC owns **92%** of Sweetwater. **[Fact]** URC brings uranium deal skills and a mostly pre-cash royalty book; legacy shareholders own about **41%** of the combined company after the arrangement closed **2026-07-27**. **[Fact]** The public parent moved to Delaware and listed on NASDAQ **2026-07-28**, exiting the Canadian PFIC profile for *new* U.S. holders after domestication.

---

## Why the market might be wrong

The market may still price this as a sleepy foreign uranium royalty name, or as a soda-ash story at the wrong point in the cycle. **[Inference]** The real security is a **levered scarce-land royalty stub**: cash today is weak because soda ash is in a deep downturn, but the mineral estate is long-duration and capital-light for the owner. **[Inference]** Mispricing can also come from **deal optics** (deemed **US$3.64**) versus **equity math after US$625m debt**. **[Fact]** Pro forma FY2025 Sweetwater royalty revenue fell to **US$59.3m** from **US$82.5m** prior year (circular pro formas), consistent with generational soda-ash oversupply commentary from basin operators. Predictive attribute: **market_structure_discount** (complex deal, leverage, PFIC history, and uranium label on a trona-heavy cash engine).

---

## Executive summary

At a **US$2.96** listing proxy (UROY last print on a 1-for-1 exchange into New URC), the base case owner path is about **−12.4% per year** over seven years if midcycle cash only partially recovers and land/uranium options stay half-recognized. That is **not** an accumulate signal on cash alone. The bull path (operator capacity growth, soda-ash cycle normalization, uranium and Wyoming land options) can still approach breakeven to low-single-digit positive returns, which is why Power Zone personas disagree on urgency. Marvin proposes **watch** until listing tape, debt covenants, and midcycle cash are clearer.

## Risks & inversion

**Primary risk:** **Leverage through a soda-ash trough.** Interest expense (**~US$35m** annualized on 9mo pro forma) consumes most operating income; a prolonged oversupply cycle or refi stress impairs equity before land/uranium options pay.

**Additional risks:**

- **Refinance / covenant risk:** Royalty Notes terms not yet mapped from debt docs [HUMAN REVIEW].
- **Synthetic soda ash ceiling:** Natural trona cost advantage persists, but price recovery may cap below prior peaks.
- **Deal integration / label risk:** Market may continue to classify New URC as "uranium" despite trona-dominated cash.
- **PFIC / tax:** Legacy UROY/URC holders need counsel on domestication path (Lemon Cakes flags strongly).
- **Seller lockup overhang:** Orion/OTPP **180-day** lockup; early release at CAD **$7.50** per MIC [HUMAN REVIEW confirm].

**Lens failure mode (asset):** Treating component NAV or deemed deal price (**US$3.64**) as floor while ignoring **US$613m** net debt and trough EBITDA. Operating IRR and component sum both say equity is **not** cheap at **US$2.96** on midcycle cash alone.
