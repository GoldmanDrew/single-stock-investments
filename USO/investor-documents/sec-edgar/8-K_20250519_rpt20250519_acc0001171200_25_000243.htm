Item 5.02     Departure of Directors or Certain Officers.

 

On May 19, 2025, Mr. Robert L. Nguyen tendered his resignation as a Management Director of the United States Commodity Funds LLC (“USCF”), which is the general partner of the United States Oil Fund, LP (the “Registrant”). The resignation of Mr. Nguyen will be effective on May 31, 2025 when he retires from his roles as Principal and Management Director of USCF. USCF will reduce the number of Management Directors on the board of directors of the Company from four to three and, therefore, will not appoint a successor to Mr. Nguyen.
