## What this business is

Vale S.A. (NYSE: VALE) is the world's largest iron ore producer and a major diversified mining company headquartered in Brazil. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$38.4 billion**, with iron ore and iron solutions segments contributing the majority of sales; Vale Base Metals adds copper and nickel exposure. **[Fact]** The company generated **$8.8 billion** of operating cash flow and spent **$6.0 billion** on property, plant, equipment, and intangibles, leaving **$2.8 billion** of normalized owner cash before balance-sheet adjustments. **[Fact]** At December 31, 2025 Vale held **$7.4 billion** of cash and **$18.1 billion** of borrowings on **4.27 billion** shares outstanding. **[Inference]** Vale is a cyclical commodity toll on global steel production, not a steady compounder: earnings, impairments, and dividends swing sharply with iron ore and base-metal prices.

---

## Why the market might be wrong

The market may treat Vale as a high-yield iron ore equity while underweighting **trough-year earnings compression**, **ongoing dam and Brumadinho cash drains**, and **capital spending that absorbs most operating cash** even after the 2022–2024 commodity boom faded. **[Inference]** FY2025 net income attributable to parent fell to **$2.4 billion** from **$6.2 billion** as operating profit halved and impairments rose. **[Inference]** Dividends of **$5.9 billion** exceeded normalized owner cash, funded partly by balance-sheet flexibility; that payout pace is not durable at current commodity prices without a recovery. **[Inference]** At today's price, the stock may embed mid-cycle iron ore recovery and copper/nickel optionality above what FY2025 filings support on a seven-year owner-cash path.

## Risks & inversion

**Primary risk:** Commodity prices stay below mid-cycle while dividends and capex remain sized for stronger years, eroding balance-sheet flexibility.

**Additional risks:** Tailings/dam liabilities; China steel demand; Brazil country risk; recurring impairments on Base Metals.

**Lens failure mode:** If normalized owner cash stays below **$2.0B** for three years while dividends exceed **$4B** annually, the high-yield narrative fails.
