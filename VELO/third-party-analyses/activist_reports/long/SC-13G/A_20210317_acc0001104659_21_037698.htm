Item 4. Ownership
   
 

The responses to Items 5-11 of the cover pages of this Schedule 13G are incorporated herein by reference.

 

As of December 31, 2020, the Reporting Person may be deemed to beneficially own 8,550,000 shares of the Issuer’s Class B ordinary shares, representing 19.8% of the total Class A and Class B ordinary shares issued and outstanding. The Class B ordinary shares are automatically convertible into the Issuer’s Class A ordinary shares at the time of the Issuer’s initial business combination on a one-for-one basis, subject to adjustment, as more fully described under the heading “Description of Securities—Founder Shares” in the Issuer’s registration statement on Form S-1 (File No. 333-250151).

 

Barry S. Sternlicht controls Spitfire Sponsor LLC, and as such has voting and investment discretion with respect to the securities held by Spitfire Sponsor LLC and may be deemed to have beneficial ownership of the securities held directly by Spitfire Sponsor LLC.
