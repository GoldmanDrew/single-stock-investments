Item 8.01 Other Events.

As previously announced, Zeta Global Holdings Corp. (the “Company”) participated in an investor conference on June 5, 2024. At the conference, the Company reaffirmed its revenue and Adjusted EBITDA guidance for the second quarter and full year 2024, as well as full year 2024 free cash flow guidance, initially provided in the Company’s earnings press release dated May 6, 2024. The conference can be accessed by following the instructions on the investor relations section of the Company’s website at investors.zetaglobal.com.
